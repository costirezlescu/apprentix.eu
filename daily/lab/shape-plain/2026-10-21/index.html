From the field Greece · 16 October

Greece lets 257 small apprenticeship-year classes run this year

The Ministry of Education has allowed 257 classes of the post-secondary apprenticeship year to run in 2026–2027 with fewer apprentices than its regulation sets.

Staffing is tight elsewhere: in the Cyclades, the local secondary teachers’ union says 130 teaching posts are still empty a month into term, and ESOS reports a new vocational upper-secondary school running with its director as its only teacher.

From the field Germany · 12 October

Germany’s minimum apprentice pay rises to €764 in 2027

For apprenticeships starting in 2027, the legal minimum rises to €764 a month in the first year, up 5.6% on 2026.

Germany has had fewer apprentices than it needs for years, the paper notes, and the minimum, in law since 2020, is meant to help turn that round. Where a collective agreement exists, its rate applies. BIBB updates the amount each year.

EU policy Statement · 16 October

Quality jobs best way out of poverty for those who can work, says Mînzatu

Ahead of the International Day for the Eradication of Poverty, the Executive Vice-President said one in five Europeans is at risk of poverty and social exclusion.

The economy “cannot afford leaving almost 50 million people outside the labour market”, she said, pointing to a first-stage consultation of the social partners, launched in July, on EU action for people facing significant barriers to work.

Research New paper · cites Cedefop · 15 October

Is overeducation lower in green industries? It depends on the measure

Using PIAAC data for seven Central and Eastern European countries and Cedefop’s 2019 framework for green industries.

Workers’ own assessments show no difference between green and other industries; the statistical measure finds less overeducation in green industries. The authors urge caution, given the broad definition of green industries.

At Cedefop Cedefop Insight · 15 October

How VET and business work together: a new Cedefop Insight

Drawing on the Timeline of VET policies in the EU-27, Iceland and Norway, it finds cooperation moving beyond work placements.

VET-business cooperation now also covers skills foresight, governance, teacher training and entrepreneurship education. Examples include France’s Campus of Excellence Industry of the Future South and Spain’s Digitalízate, where 58% of certification completers are women. It says SMEs need brokers such as chambers and sector bodies.

From the field Spain · 16 October

Spain’s private training providers push back against a draft VET decree

ACADE, the association of private schools, says the draft royal decree on vocational training limits the opening of private centres and online provision.

Its president argued that measures presented as quality improvements target private centres while leaving out public ones, and asked for the draft to be halted, Magisterio reports. ACADE says private VET has grown with demand from students, families and employers.
