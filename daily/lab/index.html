Design comparison

Four ways to shape Cedefop Daily at about 12 items a day, over one week. Every variant is planned from the same story bank, written on 17 October 2026 from the sources available then, and never runs a story twice in the week, so later days show what earlier days left. Real editions would add each day’s news, so Tuesday to Friday are thinner here than they would be.

  • Fixed shape: The one thing · Six to know · Today’s question · Diary · More today (collapsed links).
  • Sections capped: the paper’s sections, at most two stories each (one for agencies), about ten stories in all, plus a data item and the diary.
  • Weekday rhythm: Monday policy and Cedefop, Tuesday research, Wednesday the field, Thursday AI and the future of work, Friday long reads with Lives opening the paper.
  • Lives runs in every variant once a week, on Fridays, at length.
