The one thing Spain · 16 October

Spain’s private training providers push back against a draft VET decree

ACADE, the association of private schools, says the draft royal decree on vocational training limits the opening of private centres and online provision.

Its president argued that measures presented as quality improvements target private centres while leaving out public ones, and asked for the draft to be halted, Magisterio reports. ACADE says private VET has grown with demand from students, families and employers.

From the archive: how qualifications govern access to occupations

A Cedefop study dated 18 October 2013.

Qualifications define what a person needs to know and be able to do to carry out an activity on the labour market. Health and safety, consumer protection and quality assurance are quoted to justify them, but they may also defend the private interests of professional groups, the study notes.

From the archive: tracking digital skill needs in online job ads

A Cedefop policy brief dated 20 October 2023.

Using Cedefop analysis of online job advertisements, it showed the demand for digital skills across the EU-27 and argued for faster implementation of digital policy and for up- and reskilling.

Where employment is forecast to grow

Cedefop's short-term forecast for 2027 expects EU employment to grow fastest for professionals (+1.7%) and technicians and associate professionals (+0.5%), and to shrink for skilled agricultural and fishery workers (-1.6%) and craft and related trades workers (-0.8%).
