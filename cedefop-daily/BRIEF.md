# Cedefop Daily: the brief

*As given on 17 October 2026, organised. Items marked (carried over) are decisions from Daily Codes that apply unless changed.*

## The product

- A **daily newspaper for the people who work at Cedefop**, the European Centre for the Development of Vocational Training (Thessaloniki, with a liaison office in Brussels). It is modelled on Daily Codes, the UCL Codes daily paper.
- **Coverage: everything Cedefop works on.** That means:
  - vocational education and training (VET), initial and continuing;
  - apprenticeships;
  - skills: needs, mismatch, forecasts, skills intelligence;
  - qualifications and their frameworks, validation, micro-credentials, Europass;
  - adult learning and upskilling;
  - teachers and trainers in VET;
  - the labour market and the **future of work**: AI and digitalisation, the green transition, demography.
- **News from the other European agencies, institutions and people directly relevant to Cedefop's work.** That covers sister agencies such as ETF, Eurofound, EU-OSHA and ELA, the Commission, the Council and its Presidency, the Parliament, Eurostat, and international partners such as the OECD, ILO and UNESCO.
- **News about the people who work at Cedefop**, when there is any.
  - The only name supplied so far is the Executive Director, **Jürgen Siebel**.
  - No searching about staff was done in this session, as asked. Rules for staff news are in `PEOPLE_AND_PRIVACY.md`.

## Carried over from Daily Codes

- (carried over) **Every claim comes from a linked source.** No source, no story. Figures, sample sizes and dates are copied, never rounded into new claims.
- (carried over) **Every story has a one-line connection** to Cedefop's work. For example: *"Relevant to: apprenticeships · Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes"*. It is generated from data by template, never written by the model.
- (carried over) **Approval is never on the critical path.** The edition is ready to send with nobody acting ("99.9% ready"). An editor handles exceptions only.
- (carried over) **Sections are optional.** A section appears only when it has something worth running. It is never padded. A fixed core runs every day.
- (carried over) **Pacing.** Thin sections are rationed with a bank of items and use-by dates, so every week has content.
- (carried over) **The top strip has two forms.**
  - **"Today at Cedefop"** runs when the agenda has items.
  - **"This week in numbers"** runs when it doesn't.
- (carried over) **Design** is Guardian-like and must not look AI-made, and the paper never mentions Claude. See `reference/daily-codes/prototype/`.
- (carried over) **Social-media items need approval.** Personal social-media accounts of staff are never used.
- (carried over) **Lifestyle content**, if any, is a selective Friday box: small venues, no pop or chart acts, no famous names.
- (carried over) **Recycling archive material is fine**, labelled "From the archive" with its original date. Cedefop turned 50 in 2025, so its archive is deep.
