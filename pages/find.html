Find my apprenticeship

Seven short questions, all optional. We'll show which of Europe's apprenticeship schemes fit you best, and why. For young people and adults thinking about an apprenticeship — and for the parents and teachers helping them.

    Your matches

    The link holds your answers — nothing is stored or tracked.

    This is a guide based on Cedefop's scheme descriptions, not advice; rules vary by occupation and region — check the official fiche and national authorities.

      How matches are worked out: each answer is compared with the coded answers national experts gave in Cedefop's 2026 scheme fiches (Cedefop — European database on apprenticeship schemes, read on 2026-10-01), cross-checked with Apprentix's hand-checked summaries (time at work, pay, level). Country, "I need a wage" and "university access is a must" leave schemes out; every other answer only changes the order. See every coded answer side by side in Compare every scheme.
