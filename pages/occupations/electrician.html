Want to be an electrician?

Electricians fit and repair electrical circuits and wiring systems. They also install and maintain electrical equipment and machinery. This work can be performed indoors as well as outdoors, in nearly every type of facility. Below: every qualification for an electrician that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Electricians fit and repair electrical circuits and wiring systems. They also install and maintain electrical equipment and machinery. This work can be performed indoors as well as outdoors, in nearly every type of facility.

  • install lightning protection system
  • bind wire
  • use precision tools
  • use measurement instruments
  • electrical testing methods
  • splice cable
  • inspect electrical supplies
  • react to events in time-critical environments
  • keep records of work progress
  • resolve equipment malfunctions
  • electricity
  • install electricity sockets

Shares essential skills with: bricklayer (3), carpenter or joiner (3), painter and decorator (3), plumber (3). All shared skills

And 9 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: electrician (7411.1). ISCO-08 unit group 7411 “Building and related electricians”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Belgium.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
