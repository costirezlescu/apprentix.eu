Want to be a care assistant?

Healthcare assistants work in teams of nurses within the occupational fields of nursing, social care, clinical care and care of people of all age-groups. Healthcare assistants assist in the promotion and restoration of patients' health by providing physical and psychological support to patients, friends and families. Below: every qualification for a care assistant that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

Assistant-level care qualifications (aide-soignant, Pflegeassistenz, undersköterska…). Registered nursing is a higher-education profession and is not included.

What the work involves

Healthcare assistants work in teams of nurses within the occupational fields of nursing, social care, clinical care and care of people of all age-groups. Healthcare assistants assist in the promotion and restoration of patients' health by providing physical and psychological support to patients, friends and families.

  • communicate with nursing staff
  • work under supervision in care
  • adhere to organisational guidelines
  • identify abnormalities
  • monitor basic patients signs
  • convey medical routine information
  • comply with quality standards related to healthcare practice
  • promote inclusion
  • interact with healthcare users
  • manage healthcare users' data
  • empathise with the healthcare user
  • have computer literacy

And 20 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: healthcare assistant (5321.1). ISCO-08 unit group 5321 “Health care assistants”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Ireland, Serbia.
