What the work involves

Cooks are culinary operatives who are able to prepare and present food, normally in domestic and institutional environments.

  • maintain a safe, hygienic and secure working environment
  • order supplies
  • use food cutting tools
  • store raw food materials
  • ensure cleanliness of food preparation area
  • receive kitchen supplies
  • control of expenses
  • work in a hospitality team
  • maintain kitchen equipment at correct temperature
  • handover the food preparation area
  • use reheating techniques
  • use culinary finishing techniques

Shares essential skills with: waiter or waitress (2). All shared skills

And 3 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: cook (5120.1). ISCO-08 unit group 5120 “Cooks”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Estonia.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
