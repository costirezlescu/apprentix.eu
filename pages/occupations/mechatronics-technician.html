Want to be a mechatronics technician?

Mechatronics engineering technicians collaborate with engineers in the development of mechatronic devices and applications through a combination of mechanical engineering, electronic engineering, and computer engineering. They build, test, install, and calibrate mechatronics and solve technical problems. Below: every qualification for a mechatronics technician that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Mechatronics engineering technicians collaborate with engineers in the development of mechatronic devices and applications through a combination of mechanical engineering, electronic engineering, and computer engineering. They build, test, install, and calibrate mechatronics and solve technical problems.

  • fasten components
  • physics
  • prepare production prototypes
  • test mechatronic units
  • design drawings
  • install mechatronic equipment
  • automation technology
  • align components
  • liaise with engineers
  • adjust engineering designs
  • assist scientific research
  • read assembly drawings

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Belgium, Estonia, Malta, Sweden.
