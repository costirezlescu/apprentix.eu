What the work involves

Sales assistants represent the direct contact with clients. They provide general advice to customers.

  • maintain relationship with customers
  • characteristics of services
  • prepare sales checks
  • follow up orders for customers
  • guarantee customer satisfaction
  • provide customer guidance on product selection
  • product comprehension
  • process refunds
  • provide customer follow-up services
  • identify customer's needs
  • stock shelves
  • demonstrate products' features

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Estonia, Ireland, Malta, Serbia, Slovenia.
