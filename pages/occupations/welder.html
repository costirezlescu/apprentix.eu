Want to be a welder?

Welders operate welding equipment in order to join metal workpieces together. They can use fusion welding processes based on different techniques and materials. They also perform simple visual inspection of welds. Below: every qualification for a welder that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Welders operate welding equipment in order to join metal workpieces together. They can use fusion welding processes based on different techniques and materials. They also perform simple visual inspection of welds.

  • perform metal active gas welding
  • wear appropriate protective gear
  • spot metal imperfections
  • ensure equipment availability
  • fuel gas
  • flammable fluids
  • select filler metal
  • torch temperature for metal processes
  • ensure correct metal temperature
  • follow health and safety procedures in construction
  • troubleshoot
  • smooth burred surfaces

And 24 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: welder (7212.3). ISCO-08 unit group 7212 “Welders and flamecutters”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish VET qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Czechia, Germany, Ireland.
