Want to be a butcher?

Butchers order, inspect and buy meat to prepare it and sell it as consumable meat products. They perform activities such as cutting, trimming, boning, tying, and grinding beef, pork, and poultry meat. They prepare those mentioned sorts of meat for consumption. What the work involves

Butchers order, inspect and buy meat to prepare it and sell it as consumable meat products. They perform activities such as cutting, trimming, boning, tying, and grinding beef, pork, and poultry meat. They prepare those mentioned sorts of meat for consumption.

  • work in a food processing team
  • tolerate strong smells
  • grind meat
  • apply HACCP
  • provide first aid
  • maintain food specifications
  • handle knives for cutting activities
  • tend meat packaging machine
  • cope with blood
  • follow an environmental friendly policy while processing food
  • prepare meat for sale
  • tend meat processing production machines

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Estonia, Lithuania, Latvia, Malta, Sweden.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
