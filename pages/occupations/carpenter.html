Want to be a carpenter or joiner?

Carpenters cut, shape and assemble wooden elements for the construction of buildings and other structures. They also use materials such as plastic and metal in their creations. Carpenters create the wooden frames to support wood framed buildings. Below: every qualification for a carpenter or joiner that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

Covers carpenters (building, roof and timber construction) and joiners or cabinet makers (furniture and interior woodwork); many countries train them together.

What the work involves

Carpenters cut, shape and assemble wooden elements for the construction of buildings and other structures. They also use materials such as plastic and metal in their creations. Carpenters create the wooden frames to support wood framed buildings.

  • join wood elements
  • install wood elements in structures
  • clean wood surface
  • use safety equipment in construction
  • install construction profiles
  • keep sawing equipment in good condition
  • work ergonomically
  • interpret 3D plans
  • types of wood
  • identify wood warp
  • create smooth wood surface
  • sort waste

Shares essential skills with: bricklayer (11), painter and decorator (9), plumber (9), electrician (3). All shared skills

And 13 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: carpenter (7115.1). ISCO-08 unit group 7115 “Carpenters and joiners”. Related ESCO occupation: cabinet maker.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Ireland.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
