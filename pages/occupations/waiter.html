Want to be a waiter or waitress?

Waiters/waitresses supply guests with food and drinks as requested. Waiters/waitresses usually work in restaurants, bars and hotels. This involves the preparation of tables, serving food or beverages and taking payments. Below: every qualification for a waiter or waitress that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Waiters/waitresses supply guests with food and drinks as requested. Waiters/waitresses usually work in restaurants, bars and hotels. This involves the preparation of tables, serving food or beverages and taking payments.

  • comply with food safety and hygiene
  • arrange tables
  • present menus
  • work in a hospitality team
  • measure customer feedback
  • advise guests on menus for special events
  • maintain customer service
  • welcome restaurant guests
  • supervise food quality
  • assist clients with special needs
  • assist VIP guests
  • food and beverages on the menu

Shares essential skills with: hairdresser (3), cook (2), retail sales assistant (2). All shared skills

And 17 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: waiter/waitress (5131.2). ISCO-08 unit group 5131 “Waiters”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Germany, Serbia.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
