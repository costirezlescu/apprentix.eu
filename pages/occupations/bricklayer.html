Want to be a bricklayer?

Bricklayers assemble brick walls and structures by skilfully laying the bricks in an established pattern, using a binding agent like cement to bond the bricks together. They then fill the joints with mortar or other suitable materials. Below: every qualification for a bricklayer that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Bricklayers assemble brick walls and structures by skilfully laying the bricks in an established pattern, using a binding agent like cement to bond the bricks together. They then fill the joints with mortar or other suitable materials.

  • mix construction grouts
  • check straightness of brick
  • use measurement instruments
  • secure working area
  • interpret 3D plans
  • sort waste
  • interpret 2D plans
  • split bricks
  • inspect construction supplies
  • snap chalk line
  • lay bricks
  • finish mortar joints

And 7 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: bricklayer (7112.1). ISCO-08 unit group 7112 “Bricklayers and related workers”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish VET qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Ireland, Malta.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix.
