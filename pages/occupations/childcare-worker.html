Want to be a childcare worker?

Child care workers provide care for children when the parents or family members are unavailable. They look after the children's basic needs and help or supervise them during play. Child care workers can work for preschools, daycare centres, childcare agencies or individual families. Below: every qualification for a childcare worker that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

Includes early-years education at EQF 5 (for example Austria's Elementarpädagogik), which many countries count as a separate occupation.

What the work involves

Child care workers provide care for children when the parents or family members are unavailable. They look after the children's basic needs and help or supervise them during play. Child care workers can work for preschools, daycare centres, childcare agencies or individual families.

  • social development
  • communicate with youth
  • maintain relations with children's parents
  • assist children in developing personal skills
  • supervise children
  • workplace sanitation
  • determine child welfare
  • handle chemical cleaning agents
  • attend to children's basic physical needs
  • play with children

From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: child care worker (5311.1). ISCO-08 unit group 5311 “Child care workers”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Belgium, Germany, Serbia.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix.
