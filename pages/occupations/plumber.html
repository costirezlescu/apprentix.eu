Want to be a plumber?

Plumbers maintain and install water, gas and sewage systems. They inspect pipes and fixtures on a regular basis or make repairs as needed. They bend, cut, and install pipes. They test systems and make adjustments safely and following regulations. They place sanitary equipment. Below: every qualification for a plumber that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Plumbers maintain and install water, gas and sewage systems. They inspect pipes and fixtures on a regular basis or make repairs as needed. They bend, cut, and install pipes. They test systems and make adjustments safely and following regulations. They place sanitary equipment.

  • use safety equipment in construction
  • attach PEX pipe
  • prepare copper gas-lines pipes
  • follow health and safety procedures in construction
  • inspect construction supplies
  • use measurement instruments
  • install metal gas piping
  • check water pressure
  • interpret 2D plans
  • install plumbing systems
  • install PVC piping
  • snap chalk line

And 9 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: plumber (7126.8). ISCO-08 unit group 7126 “Plumbers and pipe fitters”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish VET qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Estonia.
