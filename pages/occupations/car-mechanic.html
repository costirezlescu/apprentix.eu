Want to be a car mechanic?

Vehicle technicians inspect, test and maintain vehicles, motorcycles, engine tune-ups and tyre replacements. They repair engine failures, lube replacements. Vehicle technicians replace vehicle components and evaluate warranty options. Below: every qualification for a car mechanic that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Vehicle technicians inspect, test and maintain vehicles, motorcycles, engine tune-ups and tyre replacements. They repair engine failures, lube replacements. Vehicle technicians replace vehicle components and evaluate warranty options.

  • mechanics
  • follow procedures to control substances hazardous to health
  • car controls
  • mechanical requirements for vehicles in urban areas
  • teamwork principles
  • health and safety measures in transportation
  • position vehicles for maintenance and repair
  • diagnose problems with vehicles
  • emission standards
  • hybrid vehicle architecture
  • identify customer's needs
  • principles of mechanical engineering

And 22 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: vehicle technician (7231.10). ISCO-08 unit group 7231 “Motor vehicle mechanics and repairers”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish VET qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Belgium, Estonia, Ireland, Serbia.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
