Want to be a painter and decorator?

Construction painters paint the interior and exterior of buildings and other structures. They may use standard latex based paints or specialised paints for decorative effect or protective properties. Building painters are skilled in using brushes, paint rollers and paint sprayers for different applications. Below: every qualification for a painter and decorator that 14 European countries publish to Europass, at which level, and where to look for an apprenticeship.

What the work involves

Construction painters paint the interior and exterior of buildings and other structures. They may use standard latex based paints or specialised paints for decorative effect or protective properties. Building painters are skilled in using brushes, paint rollers and paint sprayers for different applications.

  • clean painting equipment
  • snap chalk line
  • dispose of hazardous waste
  • prepare surface for painting
  • interpret 3D plans
  • protect surfaces during construction work
  • interpret 2D plans
  • inspect paintwork
  • follow health and safety procedures in construction
  • remove paint
  • sand between coats
  • work safely with chemicals

And 9 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: construction painter (7131.1). ISCO-08 unit group 7131 “Painters and related workers”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish VET qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed. No match in: Germany, Latvia, Malta, Serbia, Sweden, Slovenia.
