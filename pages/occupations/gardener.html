What the work involves

Landscape gardeners plan, construct, renovate and maintain parks, gardens and public green spaces.

  • plant species
  • nurse trees
  • horticulture principles
  • work in outdoor conditions
  • propagate plants
  • maintain landscape site
  • principles of landscape construction
  • plant green plants
  • landscaping materials
  • pruning types
  • transport physical resources within the work area
  • plant disease control

And 21 more essential skills. From ESCO, the European classification of skills and occupations: landscape gardener (6113.5). ISCO-08 unit group 6113 “Gardeners, horticultural and nursery growers”.

How this page is made

Each occupation has 'include' and 'exclude' regular expressions, applied to the qualification's title and original-language title after lower-casing and removing accents. Patterns cover the national languages of the 14 countries that publish vocational qualifications to Europass, and were checked by hand against every match on 18 October 2026. Matching by title finds most qualifications for an occupation but not all: titles that name only a field ('Electrical engineering') or a level are missed unless listed.

Qualifications: European Commission, Europass — Qualifications Dataset Register (European Learning Data, SPARQL endpoint), retrieved 2026-10-01; the full dataset on Apprentix. This service uses the ESCO classification of the European Commission. The matching patterns are open: data/reference/occupation-lens.json.
