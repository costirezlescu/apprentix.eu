Ask Apprentix

Ask a question about apprenticeships and vocational education in Europe. The assistant looks up Apprentix’s own data — schemes, statistics, policies, qualifications — and links every answer to the pages and publishers it used.

Try a question

Before you ask: your question is sent to OpenRouter and xAI (the Grok model) to generate the answer. Don’t enter personal data. Answers can be wrong — check the linked sources. Nothing is stored on this site; the conversation disappears when you leave the page. Not legal, financial or careers advice.
