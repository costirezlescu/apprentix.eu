Entrants to initial VET by training form (Switzerland)

Number of people entering Swiss initial VET (new learners) in the calendar year, by training form (dual vs full-time school-based), qualification type and sex.

About this indicator

Unit
entrants
What is counted
Source: BFS survey 'Berufliche Grundbildung (inkl. Qualifikationsverfahren)' (SBG-SFPI), calendar-year reference period, based on apprenticeship-contract and qualification-procedure records. A flow (entries in the year), not a stock; closest Swiss equivalent of 'new apprenticeship contracts' when the 'Dual' form is selected. National series: do not add to other countries' figures.
