Initial VET qualifications awarded by training form (Switzerland)

Number of initial VET qualifications awarded in Switzerland (Federal VET Diploma EFZ, Federal VET Certificate EBA, non-regulated), by training form (dual vs full-time school-based) and sex.

About this indicator

Unit
qualifications
What is counted
Source: BFS survey 'Berufliche Grundbildung (inkl. Qualifikationsverfahren)' (SBG-SFPI), calendar-year reference period, based on apprenticeship-contract and qualification-procedure records. Counts qualifications (successful qualification procedures) in the calendar year, not learners. National series: do not add to other countries' figures.
