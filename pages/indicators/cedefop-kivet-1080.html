Young IVET graduates in further education and training (%)

Percentage of the population aged 18-24 with a medium-level vocational qualification (ISCED 3 or 4) as their highest educational attainment who participated in formal or non-formal education and training over four weeks prior to the survey (Eurostat, LFS).
