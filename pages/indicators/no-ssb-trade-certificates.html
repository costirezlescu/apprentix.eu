Trade and journeyman examinations taken in Norway

Persons who completed a trade or journeyman's examination (fag-/svenneprøve) in Norway per school year, by type of training (apprentices, practice candidates, pupils) and result (all / passed).

About this indicator

Unit
persons
What is counted
Norway, national source (SSB table 09012). Completions: persons who sat the trade or journeyman's examination in the school year; time is the start year of the school year (e.g. 2024 = 2024/25). 'Apprentices' are candidates who trained under an apprenticeship contract; 'practice candidates' (praksiskandidater) qualify through work experience without a contract. Upper-secondary level only. Do not sum with other countries.
