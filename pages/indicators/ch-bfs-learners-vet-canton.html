Learners in initial VET by canton and training form (Switzerland)

Total stock of learners in Swiss initial VET by canton of the training company (Lehrbetriebskanton) and training form (dual vs full-time school-based). 'CH' is the national total.

About this indicator

Unit
learners
What is counted
Source: BFS survey 'Berufliche Grundbildung (inkl. Qualifikationsverfahren)' (SBG-SFPI), calendar-year reference period, based on apprenticeship-contract and qualification-procedure records. Cantons are those of the training company (cantonal boundaries as at 1.1.2022). National series: do not add to other countries' figures.
