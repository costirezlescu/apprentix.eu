Adults' average expenditure on non-formal learning (EUR per participant)

Adults’ (25-64 year-olds') average expenditure on non-formal education and training activities over the 12 months prior to the survey. The average is estimated with respect to all amounts paid by participants. Values are in Euros per person, without taking into account PPS or inflation (Eurostat, AES, estimates supplied at Cedefop request).
