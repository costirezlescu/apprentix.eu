Training places registered with the employment agencies (Germany)

Company training places (Berufsausbildungsstellen) that employers registered with the employment agencies and job centres for placement during the reporting year (October–September), by Land.

About this indicator

Unit
training places
What is counted
National statistic (Germany), not comparable with other countries. Counts only what was registered with the employment agencies (Agenturen für Arbeit) and job centres for placement: registration is voluntary for employers and young people, so these are not all apprenticeship places or all applicants. Occupations recognised under the Vocational Training Act (BBiG, incl. the Crafts Code), i.e. mostly dual training. Time is the year in which the reporting year (1 October – 30 September) ends: 2025 = 2024/25; values are the reporting-year totals at 30 September. The most recent year may carry revised data. Source: © Statistik der Bundesagentur für Arbeit.
