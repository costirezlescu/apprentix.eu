Employment rate of adults with vocational vs general upper-secondary qualifications (OECD)

Employment rate of adults whose highest qualification is upper-secondary or post-secondary non-tertiary, split into vocational (ISCED 35/45) and general (ISCED 34/44) orientation. From OECD Education at a Glance (labour-market outcomes, NEAC data collection).

About this indicator

Unit
%
What is counted
Counts all adults in the age group (in or out of education) whose highest attainment is at this level, from national labour force surveys; it is not limited to recent graduates (unlike Eurostat's edat_lfse_24) and does not single out apprenticeship graduates. Some countries do not split by orientation; flag B marks series breaks and U low reliability.
