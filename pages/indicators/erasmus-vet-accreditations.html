New Erasmus accreditations in VET

Number of organisations or consortia awarded an Erasmus accreditation in vocational education and training (KA120-VET), by country and call year. Accredited organisations can then apply each year for KA121-VET mobility funding.

Interactive map, ranking and trendsDownload CSVJSON with provenance

About this indicator

Unit
accreditations
What is counted
Counts new accreditations granted in each call year; accreditations run until 2027, so this is a flow, not the stock of accredited providers. Includes consortium coordinators. No grant is attached to the accreditation itself. EU27 is the sum of member states.
Years covered
2021 to 2025
Countries
33 countries and the EU-27
Observations
155
