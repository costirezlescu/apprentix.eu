Share of 15–24-year-olds enrolled in vocational education (UIS)

Enrolment in vocational programmes (ISCED 2–5) of people aged 15–24 as a percentage of the population aged 15–24, both sexes.

Interactive map, ranking and trendsDownload CSVJSON with provenancePublisher's data ↗

About this indicator

Unit
% of age group
What is counted
For EU, EFTA and OECD countries these figures come from the same joint UNESCO-OECD-Eurostat (UOE) collection as Eurostat's education statistics; UIS adds candidate countries. 'Vocational' includes school-based as well as work-based (apprenticeship-type) programmes, so this measures the weight of VET, not of apprenticeship. The denominator is a population estimate (UN or Eurostat), so it reflects both VET popularity and how long young people stay in education.
