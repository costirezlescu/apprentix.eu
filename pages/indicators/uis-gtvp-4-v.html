Share of post-secondary non-tertiary students in vocational programmes (UIS)

Students enrolled in vocational programmes at post-secondary non-tertiary level (ISCED 4) as a percentage of all students at that level, both sexes.

About this indicator

Unit
%
What is counted
For EU, EFTA and OECD countries these figures come from the same joint UNESCO-OECD-Eurostat (UOE) collection as Eurostat's education statistics; UIS adds candidate countries. 'Vocational' includes school-based as well as work-based (apprenticeship-type) programmes, so this measures the weight of VET, not of apprenticeship. ISCED 4 is small or absent in some systems, so shares can swing between 0 and 100%.
