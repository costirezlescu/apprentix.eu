Share of short-cycle tertiary students in vocational programmes (UIS)

Students enrolled in vocational programmes at short-cycle tertiary level (ISCED 5) as a percentage of all short-cycle tertiary students, both sexes.

About this indicator

Unit
%
What is counted
For EU, EFTA and OECD countries these figures come from the same joint UNESCO-OECD-Eurostat (UOE) collection as Eurostat's education statistics; UIS adds candidate countries. 'Vocational' includes school-based as well as work-based (apprenticeship-type) programmes, so this measures the weight of VET, not of apprenticeship. Many countries have no short-cycle tertiary level or report it only from 2011 (ISCED 2011).
