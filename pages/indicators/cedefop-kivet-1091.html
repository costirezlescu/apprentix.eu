Older adults (55-64 year-olds) with a learning experience in the last 4 weeks (%) - LFS

Percentage of the population aged 55-64 who participated in formal or non-formal education and training over the four weeks prior to the survey. Data do not cover guided on the job training (Eurostat, LFS).
