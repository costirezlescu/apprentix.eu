New training agreements in Danish vocational education (monthly)

Number of new training agreements (indgåede uddannelsesaftaler) between vocational students and employers in Denmark each month, from the Ministry's Lærepladsstatistik.

About this indicator

Unit
agreements
What is counted
Denmark, national source (STIL apprenticeship-place statistics). Flow: training agreements concluded in the month between a vocational (EUD) student and an approved company, covering all agreement types the ministry counts (ordinary, short, rest and combination agreements). Upper-secondary/post-secondary VET only (EUD; no higher-education apprenticeships). Not a count of apprentices in training (stock). The ministry discretionises small cells. Do not sum with other countries.
