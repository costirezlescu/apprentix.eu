Regional skills ecosystem index (RESET), country scores

Cedefop's RESET compares how well skills are developed, used and supported, combining indicators in four pillars: skills supply, skills demand, skills match and the skills environment (quality of education and business environment, education–business cooperation). Several indicators look specifically at people with initial VET (IVET): the cognitive and digital content of their jobs, their activity, employment and unemployment. Scores run from 1 to 100; higher is more favourable. These are the country and EU-27 rows of the dataset; the 244 regional scores are in the 'Regional skills ecosystems' dataset.

About this indicator

Unit
score (1–100)
What is counted
Reference year 2024; one edition so far, so there is no time series. Scores are standardised (1–100) relative to the other EU regions, not raw values, and are designed to compare places with each other. RESET uses a different framework from Cedefop's European Skills Index (ESI); the two are not comparable. Covers EU member states only.
