Employment premium for recent IVET graduates (over general stream) (%)

Premium expressed as a difference (in percentage points) between two indicators: the employment rate for recent IVET graduates and the corresponding employment rate for recent graduates (20-34 year-olds) from the general stream of education, i.e. at the same ISCED levels and who also graduated 1-3 years before the survey. Calculations exclude those still in (formal or non-formal) education and training (Cedefop calculations based on Eurostat data, LFS).
