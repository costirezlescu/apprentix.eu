Share of upper-secondary students in vocational programmes (UIS)

Students enrolled in vocational programmes at upper-secondary level (ISCED 3) as a percentage of all upper-secondary students, both sexes.

Interactive map, ranking and trendsDownload CSVJSON with provenancePublisher's data ↗

About this indicator

Unit
%
What is counted
For EU, EFTA and OECD countries these figures come from the same joint UNESCO-OECD-Eurostat (UOE) collection as Eurostat's education statistics; UIS adds candidate countries. 'Vocational' includes school-based as well as work-based (apprenticeship-type) programmes, so this measures the weight of VET, not of apprenticeship.
