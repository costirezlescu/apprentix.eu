Low-qualified adults with a learning experience in the last 4 weeks (%) - LFS

Percentage of the population aged 25-64 with lowest level of educational attainment (ISCED 0-2) who participated in formal or non-formal education and training over the four weeks prior to the survey. Data do not cover guided on the job training (Eurostat, LFS).
