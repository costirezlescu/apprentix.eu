Registered applicants still without a training place at 30 September (Germany)

Registered applicants who at the end of the reporting year (30 September) had neither a training place nor an alternative (such as school, a course or a job) and were still looking, by Land.

About this indicator

Unit
applicants
What is counted
National statistic (Germany), not comparable with other countries. Counts only what was registered with the employment agencies (Agenturen für Arbeit) and job centres for placement: registration is voluntary for employers and young people, so these are not all apprenticeship places or all applicants. Occupations recognised under the Vocational Training Act (BBiG, incl. the Crafts Code), i.e. mostly dual training. Time is the year in which the reporting year (1 October – 30 September) ends: 2025 = 2024/25; values are the reporting-year totals at 30 September. The most recent year may carry revised data. Source: © Statistik der Bundesagentur für Arbeit. Unplaced (unversorgt) excludes applicants with an alternative who still want a training place.
