New apprenticeship contracts in France (monthly)

Number of new apprenticeship contracts starting each month in France (including renewals), by employer sector and level of the diploma prepared.

Interactive map, ranking and trendsDownload CSVJSON with provenance

About this indicator

Unit
contracts
What is counted
Flow: new contracts by contract start month, including renewals (reconduction). DARES treats the last 36 months as provisional (flag 'p'). France, national source (DARES apprenticeship information system: Ari@ne and consular chambers 2013–2019, Deca from 2020, with DARES estimates for late reporting — using DSN for the most recent year). Covers ALL apprenticeship contracts, including the large and growing share preparing higher-education diplomas (Bac+2 to master/engineer, i.e. EQF 5–7), which most other countries' apprenticeship counts do not include. Geography: all French départements including the overseas DROM, excluding COM; employer establishment address. Raw data, not seasonally adjusted (strong September peak). The 'Scope' breakdown gives two alternative partitions of the total (by employer sector, and by level), so do not add sector and level values together. Do not sum with other countries.
