Share of upper-secondary VET pupils in work-based programmes

Pupils in combined school- and work-based upper-secondary VET (ED3SW) as a percentage of all upper-secondary VET pupils (ED35). Calculated by Apprentix from Eurostat educ_uoe_enrs04; Cedefop publishes the same ratio as Key indicator 1020.
