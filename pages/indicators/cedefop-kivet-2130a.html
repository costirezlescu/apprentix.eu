Adults (16-74 year-olds) with at least basic digital skills (%)

Percentage of adults aged 16-74 having at least basic digital skills. Digital skills are proxied by actual performance of selected activities related to internet or software use in five specific areas: Information and data literacy, Communication and collaboration, Digital content creation, Safety, and Problem solving. Data relates to the new Digital Skills Indicator 2.0 (DSI). More information on definitions and methods can be found in Eurostat metadata (Eurostat, EU Community survey on ICT usage in households and by individuals).
