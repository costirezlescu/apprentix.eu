Short cycle VET graduates as % of first time tertiary education graduates

Short cycle tertiary VET graduates (ISCED 554) as a percentage of all graduates from first programmes at tertiary level of education (ISCED 544, 554; 645, 655, 665; 646, 656, 666; 746, 756, 766). Annual outflows (Cedefop calculations based on Eurostat data, UOE data collection on formal education) (a).
