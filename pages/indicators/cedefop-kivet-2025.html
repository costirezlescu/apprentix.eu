IVET public expenditure per student (1000 PPS units)

Annual public expenditure (PPS) per student (FTE) in vocational upper secondary and post-secondary non-tertiary education (ISCED 3 and 4) in thousands of purchasing parity standard units (PPS) per student enrolled. The number of students enrolled used for the calculations is adjusted to the coverage of expenditure data and expressed in full-time equivalents (FTEs) (Eurostat, UOE data collection on formal education) (a).
