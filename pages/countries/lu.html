Apprenticeship schemes

Luxembourg has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Apprenticeship contract

Contrat d'apprentissage

Apprentices train for the CCP, DAP or DT qualifications with an employer and chamber oversight, in a system spanning three languages and strong cross-border participation.

Education level
Upper secondary (EQF 2–4)
Duration
2–4 yrs by qualification (CCP 2–3, DAP ~3, DT ~4)
Time at the workplace
Mostly at work (≥2/3) — 1–2 school days/week; most tracks over 50% practical
Apprentice compensation
Wage
Pay (as stated)
Set annually by Grand-ducal regulation (e.g. EUR 529–1 345/month); employer-paid, state reimburses contributions
Apprentices
3 819 learners (2025/26)
Qualification
CCP (EQF 2), DAP (EQF 3), DT (EQF 4)
