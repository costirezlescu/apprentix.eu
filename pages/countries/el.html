Apprenticeship schemes

Greece has 2 mainstream apprenticeship schemes in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

EPAS apprenticeships (DYPA)

ΕΠΑΣ Μαθητείας ΔΥΠΑ

Run by the public employment service: apprentices spend four days a week in a company and one in an EPAS school, earning a share of the national minimum wage.

CompanySchool

Mostly at work (≥2/3)

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer or the state
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 2 school years (4 semesters)
  • Typical age 15–18 or 18–24
  • Leads to Upper secondary

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 15 and 18 or between 18 and 24. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend most of your time (two-thirds or more) at the company, switching between school and work every week. You would earn a wage, set by law, paid by your employer or the state. It would take 2 school years (4 semesters). You would finish with: Vocational School Certificate, EQF 3, which does not by itself give access to higher education.

Post-secondary apprenticeship year

Μεταλυκειακό Έτος – Τάξη Μαθητείας

An optional apprenticeship year for vocational lyceum (EPAL) graduates, leading to an EQF level-5 certificate after workplace practice and lab-based learning.

CompanySchool

Mostly at work (≥2/3)

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 11 months
  • Typical age 18–24
  • Leads to Post-secondary (EQF 5) · access to higher education

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 18 and 24. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend most of your time (two-thirds or more) at the company, switching between school and work every week. You would earn a wage, set by law, paid by your employer. It would take 11 months. You would finish with: Vocational specialty degree, EQF 5, which gives direct access to higher education.
