Apprenticeship schemes

Norway has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Upper secondary VET (2+2 model)

Videregående opplæring (fag- og yrkesopplæring)

Norway's signature 2+2 model: two years in school followed by a two-year paid apprenticeship, ending in a trade or journeyman's certificate.

Education level
Upper secondary (EQF 4)
Duration
4 yrs: 2 school-based + 2 enterprise-based
Time at the workplace
Around half — Years 3–4 fully in the enterprise (50% of the programme)
Apprentice compensation
Wage
Pay (as stated)
Wage per national collective agreement, ~30–80% of a skilled worker's rate over four half-years; partly state-funded
Apprentices
43 177 first-year vocational pupils (2025); ~23 600 offered an apprenticeship (2024)
Qualification
Trade or journeyman's certificate (Fagbrev/Svennebrev), EQF 4
