Apprenticeship schemes

Norway has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Upper secondary VET (2+2 model)

Videregående opplæring (fag- og yrkesopplæring)

Norway's signature 2+2 model: two years in school followed by a two-year paid apprenticeship, ending in a trade or journeyman's certificate.

CompanySchool

Around half

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 4 yrs: 2 school-based + 2 enterprise-based
  • Typical age 18–24
  • Leads to Upper secondary

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 18 and 24. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend about half your time at the company. You would earn a wage, set by collective agreements for your sector, paid by your employer. It would take 4 years: 2 school-based + 2 enterprise-based. You would finish with: Trade or journeyman's certificate (Fagbrev/Svennebrev), EQF 4, which does not by itself give access to higher education.
