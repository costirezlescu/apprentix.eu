Apprenticeship schemes

Italy has 2 mainstream apprenticeship schemes in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Type 1: apprenticeship for vocational qualification

Apprendistato per la qualifica e il diploma

For 15–25 year-olds: an employment contract that leads to an upper-secondary vocational qualification or diploma, combining company training with education providers.

CompanySchool

Around half

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 6 months–3/4 yrs by qualification
  • Typical age 18–24
  • Leads to Upper secondary · access to higher education

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 18 and 24. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend about half your time at the company. You would earn a wage, set by law or by collective agreements for your sector, paid by your employer. It would take 6 months–3/4 years by qualification. You would finish with: Vocational qualification, professional or upper secondary diploma, NQF 3–4, which gives direct access to higher education.

Type 3: higher education apprenticeship

Apprendistato di alta formazione e ricerca

Connects employment contracts to higher education and research: apprentices earn degrees, doctorates or higher technical qualifications while employed.

CompanySchool

Around half

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 1–3 yrs
  • Typical age Over 24
  • Leads to Higher education (EQF 6+) · access to higher education

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be over 24 — many apprentices here are adults. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend about half your time at the company. You would earn a wage, set by law or by collective agreements for your sector, paid by your employer. It would take 1–3 years. You would finish with: EQF 5–8: degrees, doctorates, higher technical diplomas, which gives direct access to higher education.
