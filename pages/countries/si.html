Apprenticeship schemes

Slovenia has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Apprenticeship pathway

Vajeniška oblika izobraževanja

Slovenia reintroduced apprenticeship in 2017: at least half of the three-year programme takes place with an employer, with chamber-registered contracts and rising allowances.

Education level
Upper secondary (EQF 4)
Duration
3 yrs
Time at the workplace
Around half — ≥50% in the training company, ≥40% at school
Apprentice compensation
Allowance
Pay (as stated)
Minimum apprenticeship allowance EUR 309–495/month by year (2025), employer-paid plus expenses
Apprentices
694 students with agreements (2025/26)
Qualification
Upper secondary vocational certificate, EQF 4
