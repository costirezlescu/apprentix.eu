Apprenticeship schemes

Slovenia has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Apprenticeship pathway

Vajeniška oblika izobraževanja

Slovenia reintroduced apprenticeship in 2017: at least half of the three-year programme takes place with an employer, with chamber-registered contracts and rising allowances.

CompanySchool

Around half

  • Pay Allowance, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Formal agreement, not a contract
  • Duration 3 yrs
  • Typical age 15–18
  • Leads to Upper secondary

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 15 and 18. You would stay a student, with a formal training agreement between you, your school and the company. You would spend about half your time at the company. You would receive an allowance rather than a wage, paid by your employer. It would take 3 years. You would finish with: Upper secondary vocational certificate, EQF 4, which does not by itself give access to higher education.
