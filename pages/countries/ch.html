What is counted

All five Swiss series include full-time school-based VET unless the 'Dual' training form is selected; the learner stock comes from two BFS surveys (school year vs calendar year) that differ slightly. Eurostat's ED3SW proxy (pupils in combined school- and work-based programmes) is lower than the BFS learner stock, which also includes school-based VET.

    Counts

    Leaves out
