Apprenticeship schemes

Germany has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Dual VET

Berufsausbildung

The archetypal dual system: 1.2 million apprentices train in ~320 recognised occupations, with chambers overseeing quality, social partners shaping curricula, and a statutory minimum training wage since 2020.

CompanySchool

Mostly at work (≥2/3)

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer
  • Arrangement Specific apprenticeship contract
  • Duration 3–3.5 yrs; 2-yr options
  • Typical age 18–24
  • Leads to Upper secondary

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 18 and 24. You would sign an apprenticeship contract and have the legal status of an apprentice. You would spend most of your time (two-thirds or more) at the company. You would earn a wage, set by collective agreements for your sector or by agreement with your employer, paid by your employer; it also covers your days at school. It would take 3–3.5 years; 2-year options. You would finish with: Recognised occupation certificate, DQR/EQF 3–4, which does not by itself give access to higher education.

What is counted

Destatis counts dual training contracts under BBiG/HwO (stock at 31 December, new contracts and passed exams in the calendar year); the BA figures cover only places and applicants registered with the employment agencies in the October–September reporting year. Eurostat splits German dual training between upper-secondary (ED3SW) and post-secondary (ED4SW) levels, so ED3SW alone is well below the Destatis stock.
