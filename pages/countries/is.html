Apprenticeship schemes

Iceland has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Apprenticeship (certified trades)

Starfsnám

Iceland's route to journeyman's examinations in certified trades: school periods alternate with an extended, paid workplace contract under a licensed master.

Education level
Upper secondary (EQF 4)
Duration
Commonly 4 yrs
Time at the workplace
Around half — Workplace weeks fixed per trade; no set percentage
Apprentice compensation
Wage
Pay (as stated)
Wage per collective agreements, rising with experience and progression
Apprentices
~7 100 VET learners at ISQF 3 (2025)
Qualification
Journeyman's certificate (sveinspróf), ISQF 3 / EQF 4
