Apprenticeship schemes

Slovakia has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Dual education

Systém duálneho vzdelávania

Since 2015, certified employers contract learners directly and deliver the practical part of upper-secondary VET in-company, with financial incentives on both sides.

CompanySchool

Around half

  • Pay Wage, paid by your employer or the state
  • Arrangement Formal agreement, not a contract
  • Duration 2–5 yrs by programme
  • Typical age 15–18 or 18–24
  • Leads to Upper secondary · access to higher education

What to expect on this scheme

You would most likely be between 15 and 18 or between 18 and 24. You would stay a student, with a formal training agreement between you, your school and the company. You would spend about half your time at the company. You would earn a wage, set by law or by agreement with your employer, paid by your employer or the state. It would take 2–5 years by programme. You would finish with: Apprenticeship certificate and/or maturita, EQF 2–5, which gives direct access to higher education.
