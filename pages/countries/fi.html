Apprenticeship schemes

Finland has 1 mainstream apprenticeship scheme in Cedefop's European database on apprenticeship schemes.

Apprenticeship training

Oppisopimuskoulutus

A fully flexible route to any Finnish vocational qualification, used heavily by adults: learning happens mostly on the job under an employment contract, topped up with theoretical studies.

Education level
EQF 4–5, all vocational qualifications
Duration
Individual, per personal competence development plan
Time at the workplace
Mostly at work (≥2/3) — Most competence acquired at the workplace
Apprentice compensation
Wage
Pay (as stated)
Wage per collective agreement; daily allowance during school-based periods
Apprentices
83 988 — 26% of VET students (2025)
Qualification
Vocational qualifications EQF 4; specialist qualifications EQF 5
