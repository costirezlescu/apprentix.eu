Regional skills ecosystems (RESET)

Cedefop's Regional skills ecosystem index (RESET) scores each EU NUTS 2 region from 1 to 100 on how skills are developed, used and supported, in four pillars and nine sub-pillars. Each record is one region with its overall score, its rank among all EU regions and within its country, and its scores on the VET-specific indicators (people with initial VET: job tasks, activity, employment, unemployment). Reference year 2024.

How it was made
Scores are standardised from 1 to 100 relative to the other EU regions (higher = more favourable); they are not raw statistics. Ranks and the 'position among EU regions' bands are calculated by Apprentix from the overall score. Version July 2026, reference year 2024. Cedefop's interactive tool: https://www.cedefop.europa.eu/en/tools/reset
