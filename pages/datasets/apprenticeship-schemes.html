Apprenticeship schemes in Europe

Mainstream apprenticeship schemes with a stable legal basis in the EU Member States plus Iceland and Norway. Each record summarises one scheme and links to its official fiche.

How it was made
Cedefop's coded answers (filters and the comparison matrix) are imported directly from the 2026 fiches. The short descriptions (overview, duration, learners, pay) were condensed by hand and checked against the fiche text on 2026-10-01. The official fiches remain authoritative.
