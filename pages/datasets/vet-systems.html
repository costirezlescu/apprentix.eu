VET systems in Europe: national descriptions

A guide to Cedefop's VET in Europe database: for each country, the latest detailed description of its vocational education and training system, written by the national ReferNet partner, with links to the full text, the 'Spotlight on VET' summary and the system chart. Apprentix links to these texts rather than copying them.
