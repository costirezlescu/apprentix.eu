VET policy timeline

National strategies, laws and practical measures on vocational education and training in the EU Member States, Iceland and Norway, reported annually by Cedefop's ReferNet network from 2015 to 2025. Filter to policies that concern apprenticeship and work-based learning.

How it was made
Imported automatically from Cedefop's published dataset. Text has been converted from HTML; nothing has been rewritten. 'Concerns apprenticeship' is an Apprentix grouping: it is Yes when the policy falls under Cedefop's theme 3.9 (reinforcing work-based learning, including apprenticeships), or its title, objectives or description mention apprenticeship, work-based learning or dual training.
